The idea I wanted to show you

Make your logo wall actually say something

Right now a row of company logos is something most visitors scroll right past, because a wall of logos on its own is really just decoration. This turns each logo into a piece of proof. When someone hovers over a logo, or taps it on their phone, it opens a small card showing how many jobs you have done together, how long you have worked together, and, where the company is glad to give one, a short quote about working with you.

It stands out Every contractor site has a logo strip. Almost none of them do anything. Yours would.
It reads as real "22 projects with a company I recognize" lands very differently than a five star review from a first name and a last initial.
It works for you It turns the part of the page people usually ignore into one of the strongest reasons to trust you.

Everything on this page is sample information, just so you can feel how it works. To do it for real, the job counts and the years are your own numbers, so those are simple. Any quote with a company's name on it would need a quick OK from that company first, but not every logo needs a quote. Even just the year you started working together is enough to give a logo a card, so we could cover all of them.

This is an idea I had while working on your site. If it is something you would want, I can build it into your real website down the line. I wanted you to see it in action first.
